【DRIPPY DISC04】#02

Suddenly, Drippy heard the pounding of running feet, racing directly toward him.
It was a whole army of red ants.
They're coming to kill me, Drippy thought.
He heard a noise behind him and turned to look.
There, heading toward him, from the other direction, was an army of black ants.
A war was about to begin, and Drippy was right in the middle of it.
Drippy tried to squeeze to one side, as the two armies charged each other.
The black ants and the red ants attacked, and the battle was on.
It was a terrible sight.
The ants flew at one another viciously, using their mouths and their legs to try to tear each other apart.
The red ants were guarding their nests, and the black ants were trying to attack them.
Guards stood at the entrance to the nests, and whenever a black ant reached one of the nests, the red ant guards would fall on him and tear him apart.
Drippy was horrified by the terrible scene he was witnessing.
He had never seen a battle before.
There were dead red ants and dead black ants all around him.
They were tiny creatures, but they fought like great heroes.
One of the ants yelled at Drippy.
You'd better get out of here before you get hurt.
And Drippy moved back toward the nests of the red ants.
The guards in front of one of the nests saw him and said, You'll be safer inside, little raindrop.
And Drippy went inside.
He was amazed at what he saw.
The nest was full of baby red ants guarded by nursemaids.
Worker ants were busily building up the front of the nests so that intruders could not get in.
In one corner of the nest, farmer ants were milking tiny aphids and feeding the milk to the babies.
It's incredible that tiny little ants could have such an advanced civilization.
It seemed to Drippy that the battle between the red ants and the black ants lasted for hours.
He could hear the cries of the wounded ants, and the battle yells.
And Drippy wondered which side was going to win.
If the black ants win, they'll come into the nest and kill us.
Oh, I hope the red ants win.
And Drippy's wish became true.
Because soon Drippy heard a great cheer, and the red ant soldiers began to return to their nest.
The enemy had been driven away.
The battle was over.
