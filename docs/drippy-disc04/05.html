【DRIPPY DISC04】#05

The owl flew over the cage where his brother was a prisoner.
Drippy listened while the two of them talked.
How are you feeling, brother?
I'd feel better if I were free.
I wish I could help you.
Are they treating you well?
They feed me every day and children come to talk to me and stare at me.
But I don't like it here.
I'd like to fly home, back to the forest where we grew up.
I don't like being in a cage.
Drippy did not blame him.
He would not have liked to be in a cage either.
When the two owls said goodbye to each other, there were tears in their eyes.
I'll come and visit you again soon, Drippy's friend said.
Be careful, don't let them catch you the way they caught me.
Oh, I won't. I must go now, Drippy's friend said to him.
I'm going to fly back to the forest now.
Do you want to stay here?
I don't think so.
He had seen enough of the zoo.
Suddenly the animals did not look happy to him.
I think I'd like to go with you.
Which was naughty of Drippy, because remember, he had promised the wind that he would stay at the zoo and wait for him.
But Drippy forgot all about his promise.
So now he said to the owl, Are you going to take me on your back again?
Drippy liked the feel of the soft tickly feathers.
Oh, very well.
I'll take you to the forest where I live.
And Drippy found himself being lifted up to the skies on the back of the owl.
And as Drippy rode through the air, he thought about all the wonderful things he had seen.
He thought about the world under the ground and the millions of creatures living and working there.
And he thought about all the enormous animals above the ground, the lions and elephants and giraffes.
But was Drippy impressed by them?
Did he say to himself, what a wonderful universe this is?
With all these magical creatures living on this earth, not at all.
Drippy said to himself, I'll bet not one of them ever rode on the wing of a jet plane.
