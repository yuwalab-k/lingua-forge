【DRIPPY DISC04】#06

When they reached the forest, the owl swooped down to the ground.
He said to Drippy, This is my home.
I'll let you off here.
They were in the middle of a giant forest, not a pretend forest where the leaves of grass look like tall trees, but a real forest where the trees reached high up into the sky.
The leaves will be falling off the trees soon.
Oh, it will soon be October.
What's an October?
The owl stared at him disbelievingly.
What's an October?
Don't you know the months of the year?
But Drippy was unable to admit that there was anything he did not know.
The months of the year are one, two, three.
Those are numbers.
Don't you know anything?
I know everything.
Drippy replied, and then he added in a small voice, Except the months of the year.
Now owls are very wise.
This owl said, I'm going to teach them to you because they're very important.
Are you ready?
I'm ready.
There are twelve months in a year.
January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, and December.
Is that clear?
Oh yes, Mr. Owl.
The owl stood and waited for Drippy to say thank you for teaching him.
But Drippy said, Now I know everything.
The owl sighed.
Like the snail, he thought the manners of the younger generation were simply disgraceful.
I suppose you'll be going home now.
The owl said, That was the last place Drippy wanted to go.
The very last place.
Why would he want to go home when he was having so many wonderful adventures?
He had ridden on the back of the wind and almost been drunk by a hummingbird and killed by a giant shark and had been to a zoo and had been stepped on by an elephant.
No, Drippy thought.
I'm never going to go home.
I'm having too much fun.
Oh well.
The owl repeated.
Are you going home?
Oh yes.
Of course I'm going home.
Drippy lied.
But he kept his fingers crossed when he said it.
That's good.
Because life here in the forest can be very dangerous.
And as the owl spoke, there was a great roaring noise that shook the forest.
And the owl fell to the ground, blood coming from the front of its chest.
Drippy stared at him.
Mr. Owl, what are you doing?
But the owl did not answer.
He was dead.
And a moment later, there was a loud crashing through the underbrush and a hunter appeared with a shotgun in his hand.
And he called out to someone.
Hey, I killed him.
And for the first time, Drippy realized his friend the owl was dead.
He had been shot by the hunter.
Drippy crouched back against the leaves so that the hunter could not see him and kill him.
Drippy had no friends now.
He was alone in the huge, dangerous forest.
And he had no idea how he was going to get out.
