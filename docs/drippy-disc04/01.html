【DRIPPY DISC04】#01

When you look down at the earth, it seems as though there is nothing there but dirt.
Don't you believe it?
Drippy believed it, and in no time at all he learned that he was wrong.
When the elephant's foot pressed down deep into the ground, Drippy suddenly found himself in a whole new world.
There were tiny caverns, and caves, and passages, and roadways, and they were all filled with creatures going about their business.
Millions of them.
There were worms, and beetles, and ants, ticks, and spiders, spiders and mites.
There were snails, and moles, and sand crabs.
It seemed to Drippy that there was no end to all the creatures living underground.
Some of them had never seen daylight.
They spent their whole lives living in the earth.
Everyone seemed very busy.
They were scurrying to and fro, carrying food, building nests, laying eggs, guarding their young.
Drippy never would have believed that so much could be happening just a few feet underground, hidden from the world above.
He watched all this activity in absolute amazement.
He stopped an ant, who was hurrying by carrying a long piece of straw.
Excuse me, could you tell me.
.
.
I'm too busy to talk, the ant said, and he hurried away carrying his piece of straw.
Drippy saw a spider weaving an underground web.
Hello, I wonder if.
.
.
Go away, I have to get this web finished.
Drippy spoke to a beetle, who was pushing a small pebble in front of him.
Good morning, I wonder if.
.
.
Why are you interrupting everybody?
Can't you see we are all busy?
No one has any time to talk to you.
We're working.
Why aren't you at work?
Drippy did not know the answer to that.
What kind of work do I do?
The beetle could not believe his ears.
You mean you don't know?
Then you're the dumbest raindrop I've ever seen.
I'm not dumb.
I can count and name the days of the week and the colors.
I know everything.
The beetle laughed at Drippy.
You think that's everything?
You don't even know what raindrops are supposed to do.
Could you tell me?
The beetle stared at him, his eyes cold and unfriendly.
I could, but I won't. You'll have to find out for yourself.
And the beetle hurried away.
Drippy stood there, not knowing what to do or where to go.
He wanted to get back up to the ground again, but he had no idea which way it was.
There were so many different paths that it was all very confusing.
He seemed to be in everyone's way.
The different creatures kept rushing past him, pushing him this way and that.
