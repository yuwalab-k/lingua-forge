【DRIPPY DISC05】#01

Chapter 5.
There were two hunters.
The second one came into Drippy's view carrying a small deer he had killed over his shoulder.
I got him with one shot, the hunter said.
He'll make a nice lunch for you.
Start a fire, right.
Drippy watched in amazement as one of the hunters took out some matches and set fire to a small pile of leaves.
When the leaves began to burn, the hunter put small twigs over the tiny fire, and as the fire grew stronger, he put on heavy branches.
Soon there was a roaring fire going.
The men put a pole above the fire and hung the deer on it, and after preparing the deer to eat, they put the meat over the fire and began to cook it.
From a bag he carried on his back, one of the hunters took out two cans of beer, and the men began drinking.
It was a good idea to hunt here.
There are a lot of animals around.
But we have to be careful.
This is not the hunting season.
If we get caught, we'll be put in jail.
Drippy wondered whether a jail was anything like a zoo.
He was not sure why, but Drippy had the feeling that these were bad men.
But don't worry, they won't catch us.
This is a big forest.
After lunch, we'll see if we can find us some more deer to shoot.
Right, and maybe some more birds.
I'm going to take that owl I shot and stuff it.
It would look good over my fireplace and my den at home.
But I wanted that owl.
Okay, okay.
I'll tell you what.
We'll just shoot another owl, and then we each get one.
If they each shoot one owl, that will make two owls.
And Drippy was very proud of himself, because he knew that one and one made two.
Not for a moment did he worry about what was happening to the owls.
Drippy sat there watching the hunters eat their lunch.
The forest was still and hot, and Drippy wondered where the wind was.
Everything was much cooler when the wind was around.
He remembered that he had promised not to leave the zoo and to wait there until the wind picked him up again.
But Drippy was not worried.
He'll find me.
If I had listened to him, I wouldn't have had that wonderful adventure under the ground.
And I wouldn't have seen the battle between the red ants and the black ants.
I wouldn't have had the ride on the back of the owl.
I wouldn't have done any of those things if I had listened to the wind.
That proves that I know more than he does.
The hunters finished their lunch.
Don't forget to put out the fire.
Right.
The other man kicked some dirt onto the fire, and the two of them picked up their guns and moved off to begin hunting again.
Drippy was alone.
He could hear the sounds of the forest all around him.
Birds were singing and all kinds of little creatures were moving around, going about their business.
