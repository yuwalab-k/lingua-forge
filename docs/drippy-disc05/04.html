【DRIPPY DISC05】#04

Deep in the forest, Drippy watched as the fire spread.
It was all around him now, moving faster and faster, and the air was beginning to get very hot.
The flames were still filled with all the colors of the rainbow, but Drippy was no longer interested in the colors or in the number of fires.
Drippy was becoming frightened.
So were all the other living things in the forest.
All through the forest, mothers and fathers were quickly gathering up their children to try to take them to safety.
A family of snakes hurried past Drippy.
Slither, said the mother, and the snakes slithered away.
A family of frightened rabbits appeared.
Hop, said the mother rabbit, and all the rabbits hopped away.
A family of deer came into view.
Leap, said the mother deer, and all the deer leaped out of sight.
A family of moles appeared.
Dig, said the mother mole, and all the little moles started digging into the earth.
There were rabbits and antelope and spiders and chipmunks, and all of them were terrified of the fire.
They ran in every direction, looking for safety, trying to find a way out of the forest in which they were trapped.
The mothers and fathers herded their children, guarding and protecting them.
But there was no one to protect Drippy.
