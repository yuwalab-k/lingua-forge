【DRIPPY DISC05】#03

Far away, Mrs. Dewdrop was still searching for her missing son.
Has anyone seen Drippy?
She called.
And the birds and the animals felt sorry for Mrs. Dewdrop, and they tried to help her.
They kept spreading the word about Drippy until finally the news reached the snail.
I saw him, the snail said.
Where?
In the zoo.
Jumbo the elephant stabbed Drippy into the ground, and I gave Drippy a ride up on my back.
Mrs. Dewdrop was very excited.
At last, I found my son.
Where is he now?
I saw an owl pick him up.
I think he flew him into the forest.
And they both turned to look at the forest far away.
And as they looked, they could see the terrible flames shooting high into the air.
It's a forest fire!
And she began to cry, for she knew she would never see her son again.
Nothing could ever escape from a forest fire.
