【DRIPPY DISC07】#05

The car drove past the park where Drippy had been such a hero only a few hours earlier.
Drippy looked out the window and thought of the thousands of people who had gotten to their knees and prayed to him.
And he wondered what would they all think of him now that he was on his way to rob a bank.
He was about to become a common criminal.
They can't make me rob the bank.
I'll escape before they get inside the bank.
I'll try to warn the police.
Drippy looked up and saw the statue of the saint.
And it seemed to him that the saint looked very angry.
The car pulled up in front of the bank.
It was late at night and the street was dark and deserted.
Drippy was praying that a policeman would come along and see the men and arrest them.
But when you want a policeman, you can never find one.
The bearded man said, it looks safe.
Let's go.
Each man picked up a suitcase that they were going to put the money in and got out of the car.
Now's my chance to escape, thought Drippy.
He started to drip down the man's beard.
Unfortunately for Drippy, the movement tickled the man and he scratched his beard and pushed Drippy deep into the hairs.
The men were moving toward the bank and Drippy was trapped in the beard.
One of the men took out a pair of wire cutters.
I'm going to cut the alarm system.
Drippy held his breath, hoping that the man would fail, that the alarm system would go off.
But nothing happened.
I did it.
Good.
The man with the beard had taken out a strange looking instrument and opened the back door.
Again Drippy tried to slip out of the beard.
But the man was walking so fast that Drippy had to cling on for dear life.
In just a few minutes, all the men were inside the bank.
Everybody be very quiet, one of the men said.
The inside of the bank was empty and cold.
Drippy shivered and he was not sure whether it was from the cold or from fear.
He had never robbed a bank before and it made him very nervous.
The men crossed the floor of the bank and the man with the beard said, the vault's down this way.
And they all tiptoed down to the vault where all the money and gold were kept.
I'm doing it.
Drippy thought in despair.
I'm robbing a bank.
If they catch me, they'll hang me.
