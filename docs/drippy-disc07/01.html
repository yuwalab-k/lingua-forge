【DRIPPY DISC07】#01

Chapter 7.
Locked in the iron box, Drippy was carried out of the church and into an automobile.
He had never been in an automobile before, and the noise of the motor was frightening.
The car sped down the street, and Drippy felt himself being bounced up and down.
When he had been bounced up and down by the wind, it was fun.
But somehow this was not fun.
Part of the reason, of course, was that Drippy was afraid.
He had never been kidnapped before.
Now he would probably not be able to go to Rome and be famous.
There was no air in the iron box, and Drippy could not breathe.
I'm going to die in here.
Why didn't I listen to the wind and stay on the park bench?
But, as usual, Drippy thought of that too late.
It was only when he was in trouble that he thought about how bad he had been.
The ride in the car seemed to take forever.
Of course, it was not forever.
But to a raindrop, it seemed that way.
Finally, the car came to a stop.
Drippy felt himself being picked up and carried out of the car.
He was brought into the living room of a house and sat down on a table.
