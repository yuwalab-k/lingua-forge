【DRIPPY DISC07】#06

One of the bank robbers opened a small black bag that he was carrying and took out some plastic explosives.
Of course, Drippy did not know it was plastic explosives.
He had no idea what it was.
He watched the man press it against a huge safe and then put a long piece of string in it.
Hurry up!
I want to get out of here!
So did Drippy, but he didn't have a chance.
The man with the plastic explosives stood up.
All set!
He called.
All set for what?
Drippy wondered.
He soon found out.
The man with the beard lit the end of the piece of string and said, Everybody stand back!
Quickly!
And the men hurried into the next room and huddled against the wall as the string burned.
Drippy was puzzled.
He had no idea what was happening.
Were the men going to burn the bank down?
But if they did, all the money would burn up.
He watched as the fire burned down the string.
And when it reached the plastic, there was a sudden loud explosion.
And the plastic blew away the door to the vault.
The men gave a cheer and then hurried inside the vault.
Drippy peered out through the hairs of the beard.
And he saw the most incredible sight.
Everywhere he looked, there were gold and silver bars and money piled up in huge heaps.
That's what a million dollars must look like.
There must be five million dollars here, the bearded man said.
Ten million.
Fifteen million.
We're rich!
The men were grinning and acting like children, throwing the money into the air, laughing happily.
Drippy was not happy.
Robbing a bank was a very serious crime.
If we're caught, they'll never believe I'm innocent.
They'll lock me up with the others.
But they don't hang me first.
The men were piling the money into the suitcases they had brought.
Each suitcase was filled to the brim.
Some of the men just took the gold and silver bars.
Hurry up!
The bearded man urged.
And for once, Drippy agreed with him.
He wished that they would all hurry, so that they could get out of here before the police came.
Finally, all the suitcases were full.
Are we ready?
The man with the beard called out.
Yes.
Then let's get out of here.
