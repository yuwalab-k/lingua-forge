We'll each take a suitcase to put the money in.
After the robbery, we'll come back here to divide the money, and we'll all go in different directions.
I'm going to take my money and go to Florida, the first man told him.
I'm going to buy a boat and live like a king.
The second man said, I'm going to take my money and go to Alaska.
I'm going to spend the rest of my life hunting and fishing.
The third man said, I'm going to take my money and live in Paris.
The women are supposed to be beautiful there.
They all turned to the bearded man to see what he was going to do with his money.
All he said was, I haven't made up my mind yet.
And Drippy knew that he was lying.
He knew where he was going, but he was too smart to tell the others.
He was the most dangerous of all the men.
I'm going to stay far away from him.
Let's go, the man with the beard said.
I'm glad I'm not going with them.
If they get caught, they'll go to jail for the rest of their lives.
And even as Drippy was thinking it, the man with the beard reached down to pick up the map on the table, and Drippy got caught in his beard.
Before Drippy could get away, he found himself trapped in the beard, moving on his way out to the car with the four men.
Oh no, I'm on my way to rob a bank.
In the car, Drippy sat very quietly in the man's beard.
He was afraid that if he moved and the man knew he was there, something terrible would happen to him.
So he just sat there and listened to the conversation.
One of the men said, all of us are wanted by the police.
We can't let them catch us.
If we have to, we'll shoot our way out.
And Drippy suddenly realized what the guns were for.
They were going to shoot policemen.
Drippy felt sick, but there was nothing he could do.
The man whose beard he was in said, remember the bank we robbed in Chicago where we had to kill the two guards?
Yeah, serve them right for trying to stop us.
And Drippy sat in the bank robber's beard, trembling with fear.
Every time the bearded man moved his head, Drippy bobbed up and down.
He was so nervous that the motion did not even bother him.
All he wanted to do was to get away from these terrible men.
