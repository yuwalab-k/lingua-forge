【DRIPPY DISC07】#02

Trippie had no idea where he was or how he was going to get out of here.
He wanted to go back to the church where he was famous and have all the people come and look at him again.
He remembered the happy look on everyone's face and all because of him.
He had been a hero and now he was nothing, a prisoner in a box.
He heard men's voices.
Did you have any trouble?
Not a bit.
They were so dumb, they didn't even have anyone guarding it.
He's right.
Someone should have been guarding me.
I'm very valuable.
Where is it?
It's right here in this box.
That's great.
How much do you think it's worth?
I think the church will pay millions to get it back.
Trippie was not exactly sure how much millions was, but it sounded important.
Let me see it, the man's voice said.
Sure.
A moment later, the top of the box was lifted up, and Trippie could see a man looking down at him.
The expression on the man's face was not happy.
Doesn't look to me like it's worth very much.
What's he talking about?
Of course I'm worth a lot.
Doesn't he know he's looking at a genuine miracle?
Trippie was so conceited that he had forgotten that he was not a tear from the statue of the saint, but only a raindrop.
Close it up, the man said.
But at that moment, before the man closed the top of the box, a doorbell rang.
And the man said, Let's go see who it is.
And he left the box open. music Now is my chance to escape, Trippie thought.
He waited until he heard the two men walk away.
The moment he was alone, Trippie grabbed the side of the box and started to climb out.
He moved as quickly as he could.
At any moment, the kidnappers might be back, and he would be locked inside the box again.
The box seemed very big to Trippie, and he had trouble climbing to the top.
But he finally managed to reach it, and he quickly slid down the side onto the table.
Now what do I do?
Trippie said to himself.
He had no idea where he was or how he was going to get away.
The wind was not here to help him.
In fact, nobody was here to help him.
When the kidnappers found out he had escaped, they would be very angry.
They would find him and put him back in the box again.
And this time, they would keep him locked up.
