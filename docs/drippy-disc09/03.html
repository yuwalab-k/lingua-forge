【DRIPPY DISC09】#03

Drippy, in a corner of the room, heard all this.
He saw how unhappy the mother and father were because their little daughter was dying.
And do you know what Drippy was thinking?
About the best way for him to get away.
That's right.
He was not a bit concerned about the little girl, or her mother, or father.
All he could think about was himself.
He wanted to get out of this house and go play somewhere else.
Drippy looked around.
The window.
I'll go out through the window.
It was open, so it would be easy for him.
I'll crawl out the window, and when I'm outside, I'll call my friend, Mr. Wind, and he'll come and pick me up and take me for a ride.
And I'll tell him all about my wonderful adventure.
And with that, Drippy turned away from the little girl in the bed and started crawling toward the window.
There were curtains on the window, and he slowly climbed up them until he reached the windowsill.
Up to now, it had been a dark, gloomy day.
The cyclone had filled the sky with clouds so that the sun could not be seen.
But now the cyclone was gone.
And just as Drippy stood up on the windowsill, the sun shone through the clouds.
It came through the window and shone right through Drippy, turning him a beautiful blue color.
The girl's father took the little girl's hand and said, Darling, speak to me.
The sick little girl opened her eyes, and when she did, she saw this beautiful blue drop of water on the windowsill with the sun shining through it.
It took her breath away.
It was the loveliest thing she had ever seen.
She raised an arm and pointed it at Drippy and said, How beautiful!
Look!
And they all turned to look.
And Drippy, who had no idea that the little girl was talking about him, turned to look and see what was so beautiful.
And as he turned, the reflection of the sunlight going through him changed him to red.
And the little girl exclaimed, Oh, it's red now!
And Drippy turned around to see what was red.
And as he moved, the sunlight seemed to turn him to green.
It's green, everyone exclaimed.
Drippy could not imagine what they were talking about.
He wanted to see all the beautiful colors that everyone was discussing.
And he turned again.
The little girl was so excited that she was sitting up in bed now, and there was color in her face.
It's gold!
It's gold!
And they all said, Yes, it's gold!
What's gold?
Drippy wondered.
