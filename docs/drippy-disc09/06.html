【DRIPPY DISC09】#06

It was a strange room with small cages in it.
In the cages were rabbits.
Trippy could not imagine why anyone would keep rabbits in cages in their home.
Trippy heard a noise and turned.
The spider had crawled under the door and was coming toward him.
It had an evil look on its face.
It's gonna kill me, Trippy thought.
And Trippy turned and hurried into one of the cages with a rabbit.
He'll be afraid to come in here.
And he climbed up into the rabbit's ear.
Trippy was right.
The spider was afraid to get too close to the rabbit.
I'll get you next time, the spider said.
And he turned, crawled back under the door again and was gone.
It was very comfortable in the rabbit's ear.
It was soft and downy the way rabbits are.
Trippy was very tired.
He had had a long hard day and enough adventures to last him for a lifetime.
Before I go out and have another adventure, I think I'll take a little nap.
And he closed his eyes and in one second was fast asleep.
