【DRIPPY DISC09】#02

He moved out from under the table and looked around to see where he was.
He was in somebody's bedroom.
He had never been in a bedroom before.
This was a beautiful one.
The walls were pink and the furniture was pink.
Even the little table he was under was pink.
It was a little girl's bedroom.
When Drippy moved out from under the table, he could see the little girl who lived there.
She was lying in bed in a pink nightgown, and she seemed to be asleep.
She was a beautiful little girl, about eight years old, with soft, delicate features.
Her face was very pale.
A nurse dressed in white tiptoed into the room and went up to the bed and felt the little girl's forehead.
She's very hot.
She has a high fever.
The girl's mother and father and doctor had come into the room.
The doctor studied the little girl for a moment.
I'm afraid she's very sick.
The doctor told the little girl's parents.
Is she going to get better?
The doctor shook his head.
We've tried everything, Mrs. Harris.
You've got to save her, the little girl's father said.
You can't let her die.
The nurse pointed to a tray of food at the girl's bedside.
The food had not been touched.
She hasn't eaten anything in days.
It's as though she doesn't want to live anymore.
She must, the mother exclaimed.
