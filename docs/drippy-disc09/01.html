【DRIPPY DISC09】#01

Chapter 9 Back at the jail, the little cricket had sneaked in the back door and was looking for Drippy.
He went from cell to cell, but there was no sight of the little raindrop.
The cricket passed the cell where the bank robber was.
But because the bank robber had shaved off his beard, the cricket did not even recognize him.
Finally, the cricket had to give up.
He went outside to where the robin was waiting, and he said, I have bad news.
You'll have to tell Mrs. Dewdrop that Drippy has disappeared.
And the robin had to fly back to Mrs. Dewdrop and tell her what had happened.
Oh, my poor son is probably dead.
But she was wrong.
As it turned out, Drippy was very lucky.
The house and all of its furniture had fallen on Drippy, but a table had landed directly over his head so that instead of being crushed, he was protected.
As the furniture fell back down into the house, everything rattled and roared and shook.
But in a few minutes, as the cyclone left, everything was very quiet.
Drippy sat up under the table, waiting to see if anything terrible was going to happen to him again.
But there was nothing.
And Drippy thought, I've had another wonderful adventure.
He was glad now that he had not listened to the wind.
If I had listened to the wind, I would have stayed on that park bench.
And the people in town wouldn't have gotten down on their knees to me.
And I wouldn't have been kidnapped and been in a bank robbery and escaped from jail.
None of those incredible things would have happened.
And Drippy completely forgot about how frightened he had been, and how he had called on the wind for help, and how he had wanted to go back to his family.
Oh no, Drippy was no longer in trouble.
So he thought only about himself and about how wonderful he was.
I think I'll go and have another adventure.
