No one is going to be able to save me this time.
The wind will never find me here.
Oh, why didn't I stay on the park bench?
Why didn't I keep my promise?
Chapter 6 There is one thing that must be said about Drippy.
It is true that he was naughty, and selfish, and conceited, but he was a brave little raindrop.
Drippy never gave up, and now, trapped at the bottom of the huge statue, he thought, I've got to try to find a way out of here.
It was so dark inside the statue that Drippy could not see anything.
It was worse than being in the shark's stomach.
Only this time, no one was going to cut the statue open and let him out.
This time, he would have to save himself.
He was too important to die.
Had not the queen bee and the wind told him that he was the most important thing in the world?
That the earth could not survive without him?
The whole world is depending on me.
If I save myself, I'll save the world.
And suddenly, he was no longer afraid of the darkness around him.
Since he had fallen down the statue, the only chance to save himself was to climb up.
So, Drippy began to climb.
He moved very slowly, inching his way up the steep sides.
It was not easy.
The sides were slippery, and Drippy would keep falling back.
But every time he fell, he would start climbing again.
And little by little, he inched his way toward the top.
Soon he became exhausted.
He did not think he could move another inch.
He began to wonder whether he would ever be able to save himself.
