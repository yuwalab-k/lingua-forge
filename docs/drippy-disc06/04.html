A priest was sent for.
Now the priest was a very holy man, and he believed in God.
But he had never seen a miracle.
When he was told that a miracle was happening in the town square, he said, You must be mistaken.
Miracles don't happen in this day and age.
But the woman who told him insisted that it was a miracle.
So the priest finally decided that he'd better look into it.
When he arrived at the town square, he saw thousands of people standing there.
And they were all pointing at the statue and talking about it in loud voices.
The priest did not understand what all the excitement was about.
He had seen the statue of the saint hundreds of times, and there was nothing miraculous about it.
But now, as the priest arrived at the square, the crowd made a path for him, and he moved closer to the statue and looked up.
And he saw it.
There was no question about it.
There, just below the saint's eye, was a big fat tear.
It's a miracle!
The priest gasped.
And a giant shout went up from the crowd.
All the people got down on their knees and began to pray.
The saint has come to give us a message, to tell us that God is watching over us.
He is crying for our sins.
