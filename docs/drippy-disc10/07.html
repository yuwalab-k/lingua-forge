【DRIPPY DISC10】#07

Mrs. Dewdrop saw the bluebird coming, and in her heart, she felt that there was good news.
For bluebirds always bring good news.
And she was right.
We found your son!
He's safe!
Mrs. Dewdrop cried tears of joy.
That's wonderful!
Thank you so much!
Where is he?
He's on the stage, in a theater.
Mrs. Dewdrop was puzzled.
What's he doing on a stage?
He's doing an act with the seal.
You must bring him back to me.
I can't. Bluebirds aren't allowed in theaters.
Then how are we going to save him?
And they both sat there, thinking.
And suddenly Mrs. Dewdrop remembered her friend.
The wind will save him.
The wind can go anywhere.
And she called out to the wind, and he flew down to her side.
We found Drippy!
Was he back on the park bench?
No, the bluebird said.
He's on a stage, in a theater.
Will you bring him back to me?
The wind did not know what to say.
He was not sure that Drippy was ready to come home yet.
Drippy still had a lesson to learn.
But the wind did not want to disappoint Mrs. Dewdrop.
She had suffered so much already because of Drippy's thoughtlessness.
Will you?
Please?
I'll see what I can do.
And off the wind flew.
On his way to the theater, the wind was very busy.
He brought rain to a farm that needed it.
He blew pollen from one field of flowers to another.
He cooled off a poor family that was living in a small cottage.
And finally he reached the theater.
