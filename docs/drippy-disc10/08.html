【DRIPPY DISC10】#08

The front door was closed.
The wind blew, trying to get inside.
But it was no use.
Drippy was inside the theater, but the wind could not get to him.
I'll wait.
When the show is over, they'll have to open the doors to let the people out.
And when they do, I'll blow inside and get Drippy.
And so the wind waited.
Inside the theater, the show was still going on.
Drippy was having the time of his life.
I love show business.
It's such fun being a star.
The real star of the show, Harris, the greatest magician in the world, came on stage.
His assistant brought out the cage with a rabbit in it.
I'll go back in the rabbit's ear for a while and rest.
He climbed up on the rabbit and got into his ear.
Meanwhile, Mr. Harris was doing a number of amazing tricks.
He poured red water from one bottle into another, and the water turned blue.
He took a rope and cut it into several pieces, and presto, it became one rope again.
He did amazing tricks, and the audience loved him.
And now I'm going to do the greatest trick of all.
He picked up the cage with a rabbit and Drippy in it and placed it on a table.
Oh, good.
I'm the star of this trick.
The magician took a cloth and covered the cage.
Now watch closely.
He whipped away the cloth.
The cage, the rabbit, and Drippy had disappeared into thin air.
Thanks for watching.
