【DRIPPY DISC10】#05

Who are you?
Drippy asked.
I'm a seal.
What's a seal?
Me.
What do you do?
I bark.
The seal barked for him.
And I play ball.
Where do you play ball?
On the stage, in front of an audience.
They love me.
Well, naturally Drippy wanted the audience to love him too.
May I play ball with you?
Certainly.
I'll be going on any minute now.
The seal's coat was very shiny and Drippy used it like a mirror to make sure that he was nice and shiny so that the audience could see how handsome he was.
The dancers finished their act.
It was time for the seal to go on.
The seal and his trainer walked out on the stage with Drippy on the seal's back.
The trainer threw a large beach ball up in the air and the seal caught it on his nose.
Alright.
The seal said to Drippy.
If you want to play ball, climb up on the ball.
This is going to be fun.
He climbed up on the ball to the very top.
When he got there, the seal started twisting his neck so that the ball began spinning.
Wait a minute.
I'm getting dizzy.
You wanted to play ball.
Let's play.
And he started spinning the ball faster.
Poor Drippy.
It was very hard to hold on to the ball, but he was determined not to let go.
He did not want to fly out into the orchestra again.
I'm not so sure I like being a star.
It's very hard work.
