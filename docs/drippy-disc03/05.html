【DRIPPY DISC03】#05

Do you remember that at the very beginning we told you that this was a story filled with monsters and magic and miracles?
Well, Grippy was saved by a miracle.
At the last moment, just before he would have disappeared completely, the dishwasher stopped working, just like that.
There had been a terrible electrical storm over the city, with lightning flashing through the air and thunder roaring through the sky.
A lightning bolt knocked out the electrical power, and all the electricity in the city was out.
The dishwasher stopped working, and Grippy was saved.
The owner of the bar hurried into the kitchen and opened the dishwasher.
I'll have to finish these dishes tomorrow, he said.
The moment the door opened, Grippy slid out of the dishwasher onto the sink.
He was free again.
But was he grateful?
Did he give thanks for having his life saved?
Not at all.
He said to himself, look at me, I've just escaped from an earthquake.
And he slid down to the floor.
Suddenly there was a shadow above him.
When he looked up, he saw a giant shoe about to step down on him.
Quickly, Grippy leaped up into the air and landed on the laces of the shoe.
The shoe went up and down, and Grippy was carried outside, clinging to the laces.
