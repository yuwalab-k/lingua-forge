【DRIPPY DISC03】#08

Standing in front of Drippy was the most beautiful creature he had ever seen, even more beautiful than the butterflies.
It was a large bird with a long tail, and in the tail were diamonds and rubies and emeralds that glowed with every imaginable color.
Who are you?
I'm a peacock, the bird told him, and it spread out its tail so that Drippy could see its full beauty.
Drippy was awed.
I've never seen so many pretty.
What do you call those things?
Colors, the peacock said.
Don't you know what colors are?
Drippy thought for a moment.
Tuesday?
That's not a color, that's a day of the week.
Don't you know anything?
I can count to a hundred.
Who cares about numbers, I'm going to teach you about colors, there's red.
As the peacock said the word, he flashed his red ruby at Drippy.
And green, and blue, and pink, and violet, and black, and purple.
And as the peacock named each color, he flashed the color at Drippy.
Drippy named all the colors of the rainbow, as Drippy listened wide-eyed.
Now little raindrop, do you think you can name the colors?
And Drippy named all the colors, and the peacock was pleased.
You're quite clever for a raindrop.
I know, I can count to a hundred.
I know the days of the week, and now I know the colors.
But he did not say thank you to the peacock for teaching him the colors.
Drippy looked around and said, I've never been to a zoo before.
Be careful, there are a lot of big animals in here.
I'm not afraid of big animals.
I'm the bravest raindrop that ever lived.
Nothing can hurt me.
And no sooner were the words out of Drippy's mouth than an enormous elephant walked by and planted its huge hoof directly on Drippy, sending him down, down, down, into the dark earth.
