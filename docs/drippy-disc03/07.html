【DRIPPY DISC03】#07

In the meanwhile, the wind had returned.
He was up above Drippy, watching him.
The wind shook his head in disgust.
Isn't Drippy ever going to learn anything?
The wind wondered.
And the wind picked Drippy up and started blowing him through the air, high into the sky.
Whee!
This is fun!
Can you go any faster?
But the wind was not interested in Drippy having fun.
He wanted to teach him a lesson.
A jet plane was flying through the sky, filled with passengers on their way home.
The wind got an idea.
He flew next to the plane and blew Drippy onto one of the wings.
Have fun!
I'll see you later.
Wait a minute.
This plane is going too fast.
Help!
Drippy was in trouble.
If you've ever been on the wings of a jet plane, racing across the skies at 600 miles an hour, then you'll know exactly how Drippy felt.
He was hanging on for dear life.
He managed to roll up to the top of the wing and get next to a window.
He looked inside.
The airplane was filled with laughing, happy people.
There were mothers and fathers playing with their children, and Drippy suddenly wished he was with his mother.
I'm sure you will have noticed that Drippy never thought about his mother unless he was in trouble.
The rest of the time, he was too busy enjoying himself.
The airplane was traveling so fast that Drippy knew that he could not hang on much longer.
And sure enough, he started to feel himself slip off into the air.
As he fell, the wind who had been watching him picked him up and put him on his coattails because the wind did not want Drippy to get hurt.
He only wanted to teach him a lesson.
Well, was that fast enough for you?
Oh, yes, Drippy said.
He would not tell the wind how frightened he had been.
Where would you like to go now?
He was hoping that Drippy would say, home to my mother and my sisters and brothers and cousins.
But instead, Drippy said, Some place where I can have some fun, Mr. Wind.
Now they were passing over a zoo.
Have you ever been to a zoo?
What's a zoo?
A zoo is where they bring wild animals from all over the world.
I'll show you.
And the wind zoomed down and down until they reached the earth.
And Drippy found himself set down in the middle of an enormous zoo.
I must go now.
Stay out of trouble.
Oh, I will.
And the wind was gone.
