【DRIPPY DISC03】#06

The storm had stopped, and the bartender, who was wearing the shoe that Drippy was riding on, decided to get his shoes shined.
A small boy stood at the corner with a shoe-shine stand.
Shine, mister?
Yes, please, the bartender said.
And before Drippy could get out of the way, the shoe he was on was placed on a metal bar, and a hand covered Drippy with black polish.
He was all black from the polish, his eyes, his face, his nose, everything.
And worse than that, a shoe-shine rag was rubbing across the shoe, getting closer and closer to him.
Oh, no!
Drippy cried.
Just in time, he leaped off the shoe and landed in a puddle of water on the sidewalk.
The water washed off the polish.
And when Drippy saw his reflection in a puddle, he was his white, gleaming self again.
Drippy stood there admiring his reflection.
How handsome I am!
How brave!
How clever!
