【DRIPPY DISC08】#02

I'll take you to your cell now," the policeman told the bank robber.
And the bank robber and Drippy were marched down a long corridor that had cells filled with dangerous criminals, and two of them were thrown into one of the cells, and the iron door was locked behind them.
The policeman looked at the bank robber and said, You're going to appear before a judge in the morning.
He doesn't like people who wear beards.
You better shave yours off.
And he gave the bank robber an electric razor.
I'm glad he did that.
I don't like beards either.
They really don't look nice.
And even as Drippy thought it, he suddenly realized that he was in the beard.
The prisoner walked over to the little sink in the cell, plugged in the cord, and began shaving off his beard.
You can imagine what was happening to Drippy.
The hundreds of little teeth in the electric razor seemed to be leaping at him from all sides.
Drippy kept jumping around in the beard, trying to escape.
But as the bank robber continued shaving, there was less and less beard for Drippy to hide in.
At any second, the electric razor was going to cut him into tiny bits.
Soon there were only a few hairs left on the man's chin, and Drippy was clinging to those hairs for dear life.
He saw the teeth of the razor coming right at him.
He thought, I've got to get away.
And he let go of the hairs and went down, down, down to the sink.
Luckily for Drippy, there was a towel on the sink, and he landed on the towel, so he was not hurt.
He lay there, but before he could catch his breath, a policeman walked into the cell and took the razor away from the prisoner.
He studied him a moment and said, now that's better.
The policeman picked up the towel with Drippy on it and walked away.
Drippy found himself clinging to the towel as they went down the long corridor.
Drippy thought, this is better than being in the beard of the bank robber.
As it turned out later, Drippy was quite mistaken.
