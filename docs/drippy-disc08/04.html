【DRIPPY DISC08】#04

The plan was ingenious.
It was complicated but simple.
Easy but hard.
A cricket was going to take Drippy out of jail on his back.
The big problem, of course, was that the jail was far away from Drippy's home.
They needed a cricket who was at the jail.
But Mr. and Mrs. Dewdrop had figured everything out very cleverly.
It is not easy to break out of jail, and Mrs. Dewdrop wanted to be sure that nothing went wrong.
She did not want Drippy to get hurt.
The plan had to be carried out very quickly.
Mrs. Dewdrop whispered the plan to a friendly bee, and the bee flew to a flower and whispered the plan to the flower.
The flower told it to a butterfly, and the butterfly flew away and told it to a robin.
The robin flew to the town where the jail was, and he found a cricket outside the jail.
And the robin told the plan to the cricket.
Can you help?
He asked the cricket.
Of course I can help.
Don't get caught.
Don't worry.
I'll be very careful.
The plan was for him to sneak into the jail, find the cell where Drippy was, put Drippy on his back, and take him outside, where the robin would be waiting to fly Drippy back to his home and family.
Don't worry, the cricket said to the robin.
Nothing can go wrong.
But as it turned out, everything went wrong.
