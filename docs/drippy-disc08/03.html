【DRIPPY DISC08】#03

Back home, Mrs. Dewdrop was still searching for Drippy.
No mother gives up when her child is lost, and Mrs. Dewdrop was no exception.
She had heard from a rabbit that had escaped the forest fire that the wind had rescued Drippy.
Mr. Wind, where is my son?
The wind replied sadly, I don't know.
After I saved him from the forest fire, I took him to a park and put him on a bench, and I told him to stay there.
When I came back for him, he was gone.
Don't you have any idea where he is?
I'm afraid not.
I've looked everywhere.
Oh dear.
Mrs. Dewdrop was very sad, but she was not ready to give up.
Far from it, she kept asking everybody she knew, and the word was passed around everywhere.
Mrs. Dewdrop is looking for Drippy.
And the ants told the crickets, and the crickets told the grasshoppers, and the grasshoppers told the flowers, and the flowers told the bees, and the bees told a bluebird, and guess what happened?
A pigeon, not Drippy's friend, but a cousin of his, happened to be outside the police station when the bank robber was brought in, and the pigeon had seen Drippy peeking out of the bank robber's beard.
I saw Drippy.
The pigeon exclaimed to the bluebird, and the bluebird told the bee, and the bee told the flower, and the flower told the grasshopper, and the grasshopper told the cricket, and the cricket told the ant, and the ant told Mrs. Dewdrop.
Mrs. Dewdrop was thrilled.
She was so excited she began to sing and dance.
Her son was safe.
But he's in prison, the ant reminded her.
I don't care.
I'm sure Drippy didn't do anything bad.
We've got to rescue him.
How?
How can we get him out of prison?
We'll think of a way.
And she sat down with her husband and all her other children, and Drippy's aunts and uncles and cousins, and they talked about how they could rescue Drippy from the prison he was in.
And finally, Drippy's father came up with a brilliant idea.
That's it!
Mrs. Dewdrop exclaimed.
We'll do it.
