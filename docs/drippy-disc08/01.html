【DRIPPY DISC08】#01

Chapter 8 They rode to the jail in a police car with red lights flashing and sirens screaming.
Drippy had a feeling that everyone was staring at him, saying, that's Drippy, the criminal raindrop.
He knew that he could never go home now.
He would probably be put in prison for the rest of his life.
He could never prove that he was innocent.
Even if they let him out of prison one day, he would have a criminal record.
His mother and his father and his cousins would all be ashamed of him.
Nobody wanted a bank robber in the family.
And wait until the police learned that he was not a genuine teardrop from the statue of the saint.
Wait until they learned that he was only a raindrop pretending.
Had anyone ever been in so much trouble?
Drippy did not think so and moaned.
Why did I ever run away from home?
You can tell that Drippy was in trouble just from the fact that he was thinking of home again.
When the police car arrived at the jail, the man with the beard was pushed out of the car and taken inside police headquarters.
Needless to say, Drippy, who was in the beard, went right with him.
The police station was filled with people being arrested and questioned and booked.
They're criminals just like me, Drippy thought.
The bearded man and Drippy were taken to a table.
We're going to take your fingerprints, the policeman said.
He put ink on a pad and then pressed the bank robber's fingers onto the ink.
Drippy felt as if he were being fingerprinted.
Oh, the shame of it.
When they had finished, the policeman said, come this way.
We're going to take your picture.
They put the bearded man and Drippy in front of a camera and took his picture for the police files.
Unfortunately, Drippy, who was curious to see what was happening, leaned his head out of the man's beard at the very instant that the camera took the picture, so that you could see Drippy's head sticking out of the picture.
