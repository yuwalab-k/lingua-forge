【DRIPPY DISC08】#06

Flying past the truck, high in the air, were a number of incredible things.
A house flew past it, and a car.
Birds and airplanes were flying backwards, unable to fight the terrible cyclone.
The air was filled with stores and barns and mules and doctors and lawyers and tables and chairs.
It was the most amazing thing Grippy had ever seen.
No one will ever believe this happened.
If I get out of this alive, it will be the greatest adventure I've ever had.
But was he going to get out of it alive?
I'll bet my friend the good wind could help me.
And he called out?
Mr. Wind!
Mr. Wind!
But the wind was far away and could not hear Grippy.
There was no way to tell what the cyclone was going to do next.
It spun on and on, whirling through the sky, carrying everything in the sky with it.
Suddenly, the truck began to fall.
The cyclone had come to an end.
It had worn itself out.
Everything in the sky slowly began to move back down to earth.
The horses and the mules and the tables and the chairs.
Oh, thank heavens!
For Grippy.
The truck hit the earth with such a bang that the back door opened and Grippy rolled out into the field of wheat.
I'm safe!
And as he looked up, he saw a huge house that was about to fall directly on his head.
