【DRIPPY DISC02】#05

What a busy, busy time Drippy and the Wind had.
They helped small boys fly kites and they moved huge balloons through the sky.
They blew away black clouds and made sailboats spin across the water.
They flew across the desert and blew sand in the faces of camels.
They got behind airplanes and sped them across the land.
It seemed to Drippy that there was nothing the wind could not do.
You certainly know how to have fun.
It isn't all fun.
I blow seeds of grain across the fields and pollen from flowers and seeds of grass.
And because I do, everything grows and the earth is green.
If there were no wind, the earth would be still and hot and nothing would grow.
But Drippy, of course, was more interested in having a good time.
Let's have some more fun.
The wind shook his head.
No, no, no, I have work to do.
I'm going to put you down for a while.
I'll come back for you later.
And the wind swooped down to the earth and set Drippy down in a field of corn.
Wait here for me.
Oh, I will.
I'll wait.
Good.
And a moment later, the wind disappeared into the blue sky.
