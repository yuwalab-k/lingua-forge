【DRIPPY DISC02】#06

Drippy looked around him.
He had never seen stalks of corn before.
They towered over him like huge trees.
Two shadows passed over Drippy's head, and he looked up.
There in the sky above him was the loveliest sight he had ever seen.
Two beautiful ladies with colored wings wrapped around them like loose kimonos fluttered and danced about in the air.
The wings of one were a deep blue, and the other was a splendid bright orange.
Oh, what are you?
You're so beautiful.
We are butterflies.
I'm a monarch, and my companion is an emperor.
What do butterflies do?
We play games.
Would you like to play with us?
Oh, yes.
He was not sure what a game was, but that did not matter.
He was sure it would be fun.
He was going to play games with butterflies.
Wait until his cousins about this.
They would all be so jealous.
One of the butterflies swooped down and carried Drippy up into the air.
We're going to play a lovely game of tennis.
You know how to play tennis, don't you?
Of course I do.
Drippy had never heard of tennis.
Good.
Do you know what you're going to be?
What?
The ball.
And with that, the butterfly threw Drippy against the wings of her friend.
As Drippy hit the wings, he was bounced into the air against the wings of the other butterfly, and they kept hitting him back and forth until Drippy got dizzy.
I don't think I like this game.
Put me down, please.
We can't put you down.
You're our tennis ball.
They were having such a good time that they never even thought about how Drippy felt.
Drippy wished that the game would end so that he could get back on the ground again.
Are you going to be through soon?
Drippy asked as they bounced him back and forth.
But the two butterflies were themselves so much that they did not even hear him.
Drippy felt sick.
He couldn't breathe.
He was not meant to be a tennis ball.
I'm going to die from being a tennis ball.
What a terrible death for a brave raindrop.
