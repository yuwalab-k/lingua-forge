【DRIPPY DISC02】#02

It was Drippy's good luck that the wind happened to be passing over the flower where the hummingbird was starting to drink him.
The wind heard Drippy call out, Please, Mrs. Hummingbird, don't drink me.
Oh, I'm going to, and there's nothing you can do about it.
And the wind felt sorry for Drippy.
He swooped down and picked Drippy up on his goat tails, carrying him high into the sky.
The hummingbird, of course, was furious, but no one can fight the wind.
Not even a hummingbird.
Who are you?
Who am I?
Everyone knows who I am.
I don't. I'm the wind, little raindrop.
I blow ships across the ocean.
I make waves and tides and make the windmills turn.
I blow seeds from flower to flower.
Without me, nothing could grow.
I make hurricanes and typhoons and tornadoes.
Drippy had never heard of hurricanes and typhoons and tornadoes, but he was too proud to say so.
Drippy did not even thank the wind for saving him.
Now that Drippy was safe, he was no longer afraid.
He felt very brave.
Here he was, high up in the sky, riding around the stars.
How many other raindrops have done that?
Not one.
Where are your mother and father?
Drippy was afraid he would be punished if he told the truth.
He did not want the wind to know he had run away.
I have no family.
Drippy said.
He tried to sound sad.
Oh, you're lying.
The wind shook his head back and forth in anger.
And down below, the trees waved to and fro.
That's how strong the wind was.
You're a runaway.
You ran away from your mother and your father and your brothers and sisters.
The wind, you see, knows everything.
Because it goes everywhere and sees everything that is happening.
You should have stayed home and gone to school.
Have you started school yet?
