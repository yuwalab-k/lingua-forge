【DRIPPY DISC02】#07

The game had carried the butterflies away from the cornfield, high over the ocean.
And when Drippy looked down, he saw the frightening-looking water below him.
Help!
What if I fall in?
But the butterflies were too busy to hear him.
Mr. Wind!
Drippy yelled.
Help me!
Help!
But the wind was far away, blowing rain clouds over the desert, where they'd not had rain for a long time.
And he did not hear Drippy.
Drippy looked down at the ocean again, and he thought.
.
.
What if the butterflies drop me?
And the instant he thought of it, one of the butterflies missed him.
And Drippy felt himself falling through the air, down, down, down, toward the water.
At first, Drippy was frightened.
But then he thought.
.
.
Why am I frightened?
I can't drown.
I'm water, too.
Nothing can hurt me.
I'm Drippy, the brave raindrop.
And as he looked down, he saw something that made his little heart start pounding.
He couldn't believe what he was seeing.
For the moment before Drippy touched the water, something rose from the ocean.
And Drippy found himself staring into the biggest, sharpest teeth he'd ever seen.
A giant shark had come out of the water.
Before Drippy could escape, snap!
A huge mouth closed on him.
Drippy was inside the shark.
