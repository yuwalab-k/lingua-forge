【DRIPPY DISC02】#01

Chapter 2 Far away, across the seven seas, the wind was playing.
第二章　はるか遠く、七つの海を越えて風が吹いていた
He blew against the sails of ships and sent them racing across the water.
彼は船の帆に風を吹きつけて、それらが水を裂いて進むようにした。
He rattled windows of tall office buildings and blew napkins off outdoor restaurant tables.
彼は高層オフィスビルの窓ガラスをガタガタと鳴らし、屋外レストランのテーブルからナプキンを吹き飛ばした。
He blew newspapers in front of horses and frightened them.
And he knocked hats off the heads of little old ladies crossing the street.
The wind was having a wonderful time.
When he blew really hard, cyclones and hurricanes happened.
And buildings were knocked down and automobiles were carried up into the air.
And the oceans became as tall as mountains.
Oh, the wind was powerful, all right.
There was no question about that.
But he could also be very gentle.
When he was in a good mood, he raced across the world doing nice things.
In far off Africa, a sick baby was lying in a hut, hot with fever.
And its mother was afraid it was going to die.
And she called out to the wind, cool my baby.
Please, cool my baby.
And the wind whistled over to Africa and it blew gently back and forth across the baby's forehead.
And the cool breeze made the baby feel better.
And it got well.
And in the United States, in a place called Kansas, where there are a lot of farms, all the windmills had stopped turning.
And the farmers cried out, without the windmills, there's no way to grind out corn or to draw water from the wells.
We need the wind.
And the wind rushed over and began to blow.
And soon all the windmills were merrily spinning around.
And then the wind was on its way again.
