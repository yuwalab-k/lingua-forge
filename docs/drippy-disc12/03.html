【DRIPPY DISC12】#03

The wind heard Mrs. Dewdrop crying, and he felt very sorry for her.
Mrs. Dewdrop wanted Drippy home, but the wind knew that Drippy would only run away again and break his mother's heart.
Do you want to go home?
The wind asked Drippy.
Drippy laughed.
Home?
No, I want to have some more fun.
Is that all you think about, having fun?
What else is there?
And the sad thing is that he really meant it.
He did not know.
I'm going to show you, the wind told him.
I'm going to teach you a lesson.
Good-bye!
And he puffed out his cheeks and blew Drippy straight up into the sky, higher and higher and higher, until Drippy was sailing past the stars.
Now, when you get high above the earth, close to the stars, the air gets colder and colder, so that soon Drippy began to freeze.
He had never been so cold.
He started to feel very strange.
His body suddenly felt light.
And then, the most amazing thing happened.
Drippy was passing over a lake, and he looked down and saw his reflection.
And he wasn't a raindrop any longer.
He was a snowflake.
A big, wonderful snowflake.
He was all white and soft and glittering, and he looked as though he were made of frozen moonlight.
How beautiful I am!
He had never been a snowflake before, and he was very pleased with himself.
When I was a raindrop, I dripped.
Now that I'm a snowflake, I float.
Drippy was so happy that he floated all over the skies, dancing here and dancing there.
And every time there was a lake or an ocean below him, Drippy stopped to admire his reflection.
He could not get over the fact that he was so soft and white and handsome.
I'm probably the prettiest snowflake that ever lived.
And the wind heard him.
And the wind puffed out his big cheeks, and he blew just as hard as he could.
And Drippy went tumbling up to the sky, higher and higher, and it began to get colder.
And a remarkable thing happened.
Drippy began to feel heavy and slippery and funny.
As he blew by a shining star, he saw a reflection of himself, and he wasn't a snowflake anymore.
He was a hailstone.
A great, big hailstone.
Drippy had frozen solid.
