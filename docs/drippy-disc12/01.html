Chapter 12 It's time I had another adventure, Drippi said.
He had no idea, of course, that he had just been through the greatest adventure of his life.
He slid down to the floor and started out of the computer room, when a guard opened the door for the president to leave.
Drippi jumped on the president's foot, and the two of them walked out together.
News of what had happened in the computer room had leaked outside, and there were dozens of reporters and photographers waiting to ask the president questions when he came the street.
The photographers started taking pictures, and Drippi, who was on the president's shoe, thought, Oh dear, I hope they photograph my good side.
Mr. President, a reporter asked, Did we almost have a war with Russia?
There was a problem, but it is all settled.
Is it true that they started to attack us?
There was a misunderstanding.
Everything is fine now.
Were we sending planes to attack Russia?
Yes, but there's no longer any problem.
How did it all start?
There was a malfunction in the computers.
And the questions went on and on.
