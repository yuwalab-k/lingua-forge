【DRIPPY DISC12】#06

Grippy thought about the bank robbery, and the cyclone, and the spider, and the magician.
The wind and the queen bee and the grasshopper told me that I was the most important thing in the world.
But he did not feel important.
He did not know what he was supposed to do.
He felt lonely.
He bounced against another window and looked inside.
A mother was leaning over a crib and covering her baby with a blanket.
Grippy heard voices from the next window, and he bounced over there.
Inside the house, a mother and father and their four children were having breakfast.
Everyone has somebody who cares for them, except me.
And he became so sad that he did not think he could stand it for a moment longer.
He missed his mother, and his father, and his brothers, and his sisters, and his cousins.
But they were all far away, and he had no idea where to look for them.
It was dawn now, and the sun was rising in the sky.
The sun looked down and saw Grippy.
The sun said, That hailstone is the little raindrop that got away from me.
But he can't fool me.
That's Grippy.
Well, he's not going to get away this time.
And Grippy looked up and saw the sun.
But he did not try to escape.
He said to the sun, I don't care if you drink me.
I deserve it.
I've been bad to everyone.
And Grippy began to cry.
And as he cried, he started to melt.
And pretty soon, he was not a hailstone anymore.
He was a raindrop again, bright and beautiful, and filled with all the colors of the rainbow.
