【DRIPPY DISC12】#07

The sun was about to drink him, but the wind had been watching all this time, and when he saw that Drippy was sorry for running away and for all the bad things he had done, the wind swooped down and picked Drippy up in his arms.
Now, where would you like to go?
And Drippy said, hung!
And the wind was very pleased.
Drippy had finally learned his lesson.
Very gently, the wind carried him all the way back home to Los Angeles, California, and he blew Drippy down to the forest, right into his mother's arms.
And Drippy's mother was so happy she cried.
And Drippy's father and his uncles and his aunts and his cousins gathered around Drippy, and they said, tell us all about your adventures.
And Drippy looked at them, and he thought, if I tell them all the wonderful things that have happened to me, they'll feel sorry that they didn't happen to them.
So all Drippy said was, I didn't have any adventures.
When the word got around the forest that Drippy had returned, everyone was very happy.
The ants told the crickets, and the crickets told the birds, and the birds told the trees, and the whole forest sang out with his name.
There was one thing that still bothered Drippy.
The wind told me that I was the most important thing in the world, that without me no one could live.
And his father said, the wind was right, because you're made of water, and without water nothing could live.
You're just one little drop, Drippy, but when the whole family is together, there is nothing more important.
Drippy never ran away again.
If you want to meet him sometime, all you have to do is to go outside when it is raining.
You can always see Drippy with his mother, and his father, and his brothers, and his sisters, and his cousins.
He works very hard making the grass grow green, and feeding the thirsty flowers, and helping the yellow wheat grow tall and strong, like all good raindrops should.
Thank you for watching!
