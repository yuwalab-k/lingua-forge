Chapter 11, Drippy had no idea where he was.
One moment he was on the stage in front of a huge audience and the next moment the stage and the audience had disappeared.
Or at least it seemed that way to Drippy.
What had actually happened, of course, was that Drippy had disappeared.
The table that the magician had put the rabbit on was directly over a trap door that went down to the basement.
And when the magician had covered the cage with a cloth, the trap door was open so that the cage, the rabbit and Drippy dropped down to the basement.
It was dark down there and Drippy said, I've got to get out of here.
He crawled out of the rabbit's ear and started upstairs.
